## Links to redirects

There are 437 grouped source-page/target rows covering 439 occurrences.

| Target | Status | Final URL | Occurrences | Source pages |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| `/seo/st-catharines` | 301 | `/` | 218 | 217 |
| `/seo/niagara-falls` | 301 | `/` | 218 | 217 |
| `/posts/common-seo-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them/` | 308 | `/posts/common-seo-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them` | 2 | 2 |
| `/posts/how-seo-upgrades-business/` | 308 | `/posts/how-seo-upgrades-business` | 1 | 1 |

The two city redirects account for 436 of the 439 occurrences. They are caused by:

- `components/Footer/footer.js:9-10`, which links every page's footer to the deleted St. Catharines and Niagara Falls SEO pages.
- `src/pages/index.js:64-65`, which repeats the same links on the homepage.

Those city pages were removed and now `301` to the homepage. The footer still presents them as city destinations, so every page sends link equity and users into a homepage redirect.

The three trailing-slash links are content typo/consistency issues:

- `posts/web-design-impacts-content-marketing.md:40` links to both `/posts/common-seo-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them/` and `/posts/how-seo-upgrades-business/`.
- `posts/how-seo-upgrades-business.md:4,8,54` uses the trailing-slash form in frontmatter and body.

The post template canonical itself is not trailing-slash; it uses `https://infused.agency/posts/<id>` in `src/pages/posts/[id].js`. The trailing-slash links therefore hit Next's `308` normalisation.

The complete grouped audit is in `link-audit.csv`. It includes source page, target, status, final URL, source file/line, classification, occurrence count, and distinct hrefs.

## Redirect configuration findings

These are covered in detail in `02-seo-config.md`; the link-level consequences are:

- 30 configured redirects terminate at `404`.
- 5 redirect chains exist; 4 end at `200`, 1 ends at `404`.
- 1 duplicate redirect source exists.
- 2 redirect sources contain literal spaces and return `404` on the encoded request: `/ecommerce-web-design-St. Catharines` and `/wordpress-developer-St. Catharines`.

The migration must not treat `next.config.js` as a safe copy-and-paste list. The current list includes obsolete blog targets, malformed sources, a duplicate, and chains.
